## 1. MID-GEM-01 · 12 debt payoff cases worked to the cent

**Due:** 2026-11-09 · **State:** now

**Why:** Midas is getting a payoff planner (avalanche, snowball and Joseph's own order). Claude checks its engine against cases worked by someone else. No real balances: every case is made up.

**Do this:**
1. Make 12 made-up cases: 4 avalanche, 4 snowball, 4 'custom order'. Each has 2 to 6 debts (name, balance, APR %, minimum payment) and one monthly budget at least the sum of the minimums.
2. Rules, exactly: each month, interest = balance x APR / 12 / 100, rounded to the cent (half up); add it to the balance; pay every minimum (or the whole balance if smaller); the rest of the budget goes to the target debt (avalanche = highest APR, snowball = lowest balance, custom = the order you list); when a debt hits 0 its minimum joins the rest next month.
3. Work every case month by month until every balance is 0.
4. At least 3 cases must run longer than 36 months and at least 2 must have a debt paid off in the same month another target changes.

**Make:** `midas_gemini_payoff-cases_{YYYYMMDD-HHMM}.json`
**Save in:** Prometheus Hub/Pillars/Midas Ledger/incoming (AI drop files)/
**Shape:** `[{"case":1,"method":"avalanche","budget":900,"debts":[{"name":"Card A","balance":4210.55,"apr":24.99,"min":95}],"months":[{"m":1,"interest":{"Card A":87.68},"paid":{"Card A":900},"balance":{"Card A":3398.23}}],"payoff_month":{"Card A":6},"total_interest":312.40}] (12 objects)`

**How many:** 12

**Done when:** 12 cases, every month listed, and Claude's engine gets the same balance for every debt in every month.

**How Claude checks it:**
- Claude runs all 12 cases through its own engine; a balance more than $0.01 off in any month makes that case wrong.
- Claude checks the 36-month and same-month conditions.

**Not done (sent back) if:**
- a case whose months stop before every balance is 0
- a month that does not follow the rules above
- fewer than 12 cases
