## 1. MID-GRK-01 · Give every debt in the book its APR, minimum, due day, limit and promo end

**Due:** 2026-11-09 · **State:** now

**Why:** The new Debt screen plans the way out: it needs, for every card and loan, the numbers the bank shows. Without them it can only show balances.

**Read first:**
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jpavia10/midas-ledger/main/README.md
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jpavia10/midas-ledger/main/docs/BOOK_SCHEMA.md

**Do this:**
1. For every card and loan in the private ledger, add: apr (percent), min_payment, due_day (1-31), credit_limit (cards), promo_apr and promo_end (YYYY-MM-DD) when there is one, as_of (the statement date you read them from).
2. Read them from the statements and alerts in Joseph's email or the lender's site, never from memory.
3. Rebuild and encrypt the book with publish.sh as you do now; the plaintext never goes to Drive, Discord or GitHub.

**Make:** `the encrypted book (index.html #vault) via publish.sh`
**Save in:** jpavia10/midas-ledger, a branch grok/... and a pull request
**Shape:** `docs/BOOK_SCHEMA.md section 'debts'`

**How many:** 1

**Done when:** On the Debt screen, no card or loan shows 'APR missing' or 'minimum missing'.

**How Claude checks it:**
- Joseph opens the Debt screen; the app lists every debt missing a field.
- test/book.test.mjs checks the shape of every debt.

**Not done (sent back) if:**
- a number guessed or carried over without an as_of date
- plaintext balances anywhere outside the encrypted book
