# Impulse responses: sources and licence

Recorded spaces the hub's sound runs through (2026-11-25). Copied unchanged from Titan Reliquary (`audio/scape/ir/`, whose CREDITS.md has
the full record). **Open Acoustic Impulse Response Library (OpenAIR)**, Audiolab, University of York (Damian Murphy et al.),
https://www.openair.hosted.york.ac.uk/ , licence **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Changes (Titan's): B-format W/Y decoded to stereo, leading silence trimmed, 48 kHz, at most 9 s, Opus (webm) and AAC (m4a).
