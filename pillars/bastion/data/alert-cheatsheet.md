**Channels**

- NWS forecast + alerts for mountain communities (zone CAZ336, office HNX Hanford): https://api.weather.gov/zones/forecast/CAZ336
- NWS fire weather zone CAZ597 "Fort Tejon" (Red Flag lives here, not in CAZ336): https://api.weather.gov/alerts/active?zone=CAZ597
- ReadyKern (Kern County mass notification): https://kerncountyfire.org/education-safety/ready-kern/ — Caller ID always begins with 661; confirm by listening and pressing 1; questions readykern@kerncountyfire.org or 211; opt-out by calling (661) 873-2602 (discouraged).
- Genasys Protect evacuation zones: https://protect.genasys.com/ — Kern zones live since Aug 18, 2023; look up your address, write the zone name down; ReadyKern alerts use zone names; app sends critical alerts.
- PG&E PSPS updates + 7-day PSPS forecast: https://pgealerts.alerts.pge.com/psps-updates/
- PG&E outage/PSPS alerts (account holders; address alerts for non-account holders): https://www.pge.com/en/account/manage-my-account/online-account-preferences/outage-alerts.html
- Caltrans chain controls / road conditions: https://dot.ca.gov/travel/winter-driving-tips/chain-controls — CHIN 800-427-7623; QuickMap; R1/R2/R3 chain requirements; max 25–30 mph with chains.
- MyShake earthquake early warning app: https://myshake.berkeley.edu/
- AirNow (smoke/AQI): https://www.airnow.gov/
- CAL FIRE incidents: https://www.fire.ca.gov/incidents

Sources: IEM VTEC archive (counts), weather.gov/safety definitions, kerncountyfire.org, PG&E, Caltrans. CAC029 flood counts are county-wide polygons.
